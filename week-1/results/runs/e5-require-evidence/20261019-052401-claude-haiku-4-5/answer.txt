## 3. How long does PetClinic keep the vet list in its cache?

**VERIFIED** – **No time limit** — it has no size limit and no expiry.

The cache is configured in `CacheConfiguration.java:49-50`:

## 4. Jean Coleman already has a pet called Max. Can she add another pet called "max"?

**VERIFIED** – **No, she cannot.**

The schema enforces a **unique constraint** on `(owner_id, name)` — `db/h2/schema.sql:55`:
```sql
ALTER TABLE pets ADD CONSTRAINT unique_owner_pet_name UNIQUE (owner_id, name);
```

Additionally, the `name` column is defined as `VARCHAR_IGNORECASE` (line 48), so "Max" and "max" are treated as the **same name** for uniqueness purposes.

Confirmed in sample data: `db/h2/data.sql:30` shows Jean Coleman (owner_id 6), and line 43 shows she already has a pet named 'Max'.

## 5. You search owners for "davis", all lower case. What do you see?

**VERIFIED** – You will see **both Betty Davis and Harold Davis** (2 results).

The README (line 67) claims "Owner search is case-sensitive" — this is **wrong**.

The `last_name` column in the owners table is defined as `VARCHAR_IGNORECASE` (`db/h2/schema.sql:39`), making the search **case-insensitive**. The search uses `OwnerRepository.findByLastNameStartingWith()` which becomes a LIKE query. With VARCHAR_IGNORECASE, searching for "davis" matches "Davis" regardless of case.
