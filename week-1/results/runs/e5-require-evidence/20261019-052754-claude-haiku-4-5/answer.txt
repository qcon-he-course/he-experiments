6. **Add to collection** → Calls `pet.addVisit(visit)` (Owner.java:185)
7. **Visit added** → `Pet.addVisit()` (Pet.java:81) adds visit to the visits collection
8. **Persist to database** → `owners.save(owner)` (VisitController.java:109) saves the Owner
9. **Cascading insert** → JPA's `@OneToMany(cascade = CascadeType.ALL)` (Pet.java:56) cascades the save to pets, then to visits (Owner.java:67)
10. **Database row created** → Visit row inserted into `visits` table with `pet_id`, `visit_date`, and `description`
